# Rates & Work

## Key ideas

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $$\text{Distance} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time} \qquad \text{Work} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time}$$

### Average speed

Average speed $= \dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$. This is **not** the average of the speeds.

> [!WARNING]
> Going 30 mph out and 60 mph back over the same distance $d$: total time $= \tfrac{d}{30} + \tfrac{d}{60} = \tfrac{d}{20}$, so the average speed is $\tfrac{2d}{d/20} = 40$ mph, not 45.

### Combined work

Rates add. If A finishes a job in $a$ hours and B finishes it in $b$ hours, then together:

$$\frac1a + \frac1b = \frac1t \quad\Rightarrow\quad t = \frac{ab}{a+b}$$

### Relative speed

Objects moving **toward** each other close the gap at $r_1 + r_2$. Moving in the **same direction**, the gap changes at $|r_1 - r_2|$.

## Worked example

**Problem.** Pipe A fills a tank in 6 hours and pipe B in 3 hours. A drain empties the full tank in 4 hours. With all three open, how long does it take to fill the tank?

**Solution.** Net rate $= \tfrac16 + \tfrac13 - \tfrac14 = \tfrac{2 + 4 - 3}{12} = \tfrac{3}{12} = \tfrac14$ tank per hour. Time $=$ **4 hours**.

## Practice

1. Two trains 300 miles apart travel toward each other at 70 and 80 mph. When do they meet?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Closing speed is 150 mph, so $300 / 150 =$ **2 hours**.

</details>

2. Alice paints a room in 4 hours, and Alice and Bob together paint it in 3 hours. How long does Bob take alone?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\tfrac1B = \tfrac13 - \tfrac14 = \tfrac1{12}$, so Bob takes **12 hours**.

</details>
