# Ratios & Proportions

## Key ideas

A ratio $a : b$ means the quantities are $ak$ and $bk$ for some positive multiplier $k$. The total is $(a+b)k$.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Multiplier method:** if boys : girls $= 3 : 5$, then boys $= 3k$, girls $= 5k$, total $= 8k$. The total must be a multiple of 8.

### Combining ratios

To combine $a : b = 2 : 3$ and $b : c = 4 : 5$, make the shared term match. The LCM of 3 and 4 is 12:

$$a : b : c = 8 : 12 : 15$$

> [!WARNING]
> Distinguish **part-to-part** ($3:5$) from **part-to-whole** ($3:8$). Many wrong answer choices are the other one.

## Worked example

**Problem.** A jar has red and blue marbles in the ratio $4 : 7$. After 6 red marbles are added, the ratio becomes $2 : 3$. How many blue marbles are there?

**Solution.** Let red $= 4k$ and blue $= 7k$. Then $\dfrac{4k + 6}{7k} = \dfrac{2}{3}$, so $12k + 18 = 14k$, which gives $k = 9$. Blue $= 63$.

## Practice

1. Three partners split \$5,400 profit in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$. What is the largest share?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$k = 5400 / 9 = 600$. The largest share is $4 \times 600 =$ **\$2,400**.

</details>

2. If $x : y = 3 : 4$, what is $\dfrac{2x + y}{x + 2y}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Let $x = 3$, $y = 4$: $\dfrac{6 + 4}{3 + 8} = \dfrac{10}{11}$.

</details>

3. In a class, boys : girls $= 3 : 5$. If there are 16 more girls than boys, how many students are there?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Boys $= 3k$, girls $= 5k$, so $5k - 3k = 2k = 16$ and $k = 8$. Total $= 8k =$ **64**.

</details>

4. If $x : y = 5 : 2$ and $y : z = 3 : 4$, what is $x : y : z$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Match $y$: the LCM of 2 and 3 is 6, so $x : y = 15 : 6$ and $y : z = 6 : 8$. Therefore $x : y : z =$ **$15 : 6 : 8$**.

</details>

5. In a survey, 3 of every 8 respondents chose option A and the rest chose option B. If 120 people responded, how many chose B?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Part-to-whole: A $= \tfrac38 \times 120 = 45$, so B $= 120 - 45 =$ **75**.

</details>
