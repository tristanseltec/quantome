# Fractions, Decimals & Percents

## Key ideas

### Conversions to know cold

| Fraction | Decimal | Percent |
|---|---|---|
| $\tfrac12$ | 0.5 | 50% |
| $\tfrac13$ | 0.333… | 33.3% |
| $\tfrac14$ | 0.25 | 25% |
| $\tfrac15$ | 0.2 | 20% |
| $\tfrac16$ | 0.1666… | 16.7% |
| $\tfrac18$ | 0.125 | 12.5% |
| $\tfrac19$ | 0.111… | 11.1% |

### Percent change

> [!IMPORTANT]
> $$\text{Percent change} = \frac{\text{new} - \text{old}}{\text{old}} \times 100\%$$

An increase of $p\%$ multiplies a value by $\left(1 + \tfrac{p}{100}\right)$, and a decrease of $p\%$ multiplies it by $\left(1 - \tfrac{p}{100}\right)$.

### Successive percents

Multiply the factors. Don't add the percents.

$$+20\% \text{ then } -20\%: \quad 1.2 \times 0.8 = 0.96 \Rightarrow 4\% \text{ net decrease}$$

> [!WARNING]
> A 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease does **not** return to the start. It gives $1.5 \times 0.5 = 0.75$, a 25% loss.

> [!TIP]
> Pick 100 as the starting value in percent problems. Every percent then becomes a simple number.

## Worked example

**Problem.** A price is raised by 25%, and then the new price is reduced by $x\%$, bringing it back to the original. What is $x$?

**Solution.** Start at 100. After the raise the price is 125. To return to 100 you remove 25 out of 125, which is $\tfrac{25}{125} = 20\%$. **$x = 20$.**

## Practice

1. What is 15% of 40% of 300?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$0.15 \times 0.4 \times 300 = 0.15 \times 120 = 18$.

</details>

2. A population grows 10% per year for 2 years. By what percent did it grow in total?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$1.1^2 = 1.21$, so the population grew **21%**.

</details>

3. Which is larger: $\tfrac{7}{9}$ or $\tfrac{11}{14}$?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

Cross-multiply: $7 \times 14 = 98$ and $11 \times 9 = 99$. Since $98 < 99$, $\tfrac{7}{9} < \tfrac{11}{14}$. So **$\tfrac{11}{14}$** is larger.

</details>

4. A jacket's price falls from \$80 to \$60. By what percent did it fall?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

$\dfrac{80 - 60}{80} \times 100\% = 25\%$, so the price fell by **25%**.

</details>

5. A coat is sold at 20% off for \$64. What was the original price?

<details>
<summary>Answer</summary>

The sale price is 0.8 of the original, so the original is $64 / 0.8 =$ **\$80**.

</details>
